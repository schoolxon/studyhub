# 01 — Market & User Research

## 1. Market size (India)

| Layer | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Paid self-study libraries / reading rooms | ~1,00,000+ (aur badh rahe hain) |
| Average seats per library | 60-150 |
| Average monthly revenue per library | ₹60,000 - ₹3,00,000 |
| Willing to pay for software | Realistically 15-20% (~15,000-20,000 libraries) |
| Achievable in 3 years (1-2% share) | 1,000-2,000 customers = ₹1-2 crore ARR |

Growth drivers: competitive exam boom (SSC/UPSC/NEET/Banking), ghar pe padhne ki jagah
nahi, coaching hubs ka expansion tier-2/3 me.

## 2. Customer segments

### Segment A — Single library, 50-120 seats (PRIMARY)
- Owner khud counter pe baithta hai ya ek manager
- Register/Excel use karta hai
- Pain: fees due tracking, reminders, seat confusion
- Budget: ₹500-1,000/month — isse zyada me sochega
- **Ye tumhara bread & butter hai**

### Segment B — 2-4 branches, 200-500 seats
- Professional setup, staff hai
- Pain: branch-wise reporting, staff accountability, cash leakage
- Budget: ₹2,000-5,000/month
- Higher LTV, lower churn — inko chase karo Phase 2 me

### Segment C — Chains / franchise (10+ branches)
- Enterprise deal, custom requirement, white-label chahiye
- Budget: ₹10,000+/month
- Sales cycle lamba, par ek deal = 20 chhote customers

## 3. Buyer personas

**Rajesh, 42, library owner, Ambala**
- 90 seats, 3 shifts, ₹1.2 lakh/month revenue
- Register me entry karta hai, mahine ke end me ₹10-15k due bhool jaata hai
- WhatsApp pe ek ek ko message bhejta hai — 2 ghante lagte hain
- English theek-thaak, Hindi comfortable
- Software se darta hai — "data kho gaya to?"
- **Trigger to buy:** ek din 3 students ne same seat claim ki

**Priya, 26, manager, Chandigarh (2 branches)**
- Roz 15-20 admission/renewal handle karti hai
- Owner ko report banake bhejti hai — Excel me 1 ghanta roz
- Tech-savvy, app chahti hai
- **Trigger to buy:** owner ne bola "roz ka collection mujhe phone pe dikhna chahiye"

## 4. Competitor landscape

| Type | Examples | Weakness — tumhara opening |

| Generic gym/club software | Multiple SaaS | Seat sharing nahi, shift concept nahi |
| Excel + WhatsApp | Sabse bada competitor | Free hai — tumhe 10x better hona padega |

**Positioning statement:**
> "Register aur Excel se 10 guna aasan. Hindi me. WhatsApp reminder automatic.
> Roz ki collection mobile pe. ₹499/mahina se."

## 5. Validation plan — coding se PEHLE

10 library owners se milo. Ye exact sawaal poocho (jawab likh lo, recording lo):

1. Aap abhi students ka record kaise rakhte ho? (dikhao mujhe)
2. Pichle mahine kitne students ki fees due reh gayi thi? Kaise pata chalta hai?
3. Reminder kaise bhejte ho? Kitna time lagta hai?
4. Seat allot karne me kabhi confusion hua hai? Kya hua tha?
5. Attendance lete ho? Kyun / kyun nahi?
6. Mahine ka actual profit nikalte ho? Kaise?
7. Staff par pura bharosa hai cash ka?
8. Koi software try kiya tha? Kyun chhod diya?
9. Agar ek app ye sab kar de to kitna dena chahoge mahine ka?
10. Abhi paise dene ko tayyar ho? (Ye sawaal zaroor poocho — "haan bahut acha hai" ka
    koi matlab nahi, "kitna doge" ka matlab hai)

**Green signal:** 10 me se 6+ bole ki ₹500-1000 dene ko tayyar hain aur wo apna register
dikhane ko ready hain.

**Red signal:** sab "acha hai, bana lo, dekh lenge" bolein — matlab pain kam hai, deeper
niche dhoondo.
