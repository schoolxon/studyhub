# 15 — Go-To-Market & Sales Playbook

## 1. Strategy: ek sheher jeeto, phir agla

Poore India me ek saath mat phailo. Ek sheher me 60-70% libraries capture karo. Kyun:
- Owners aapas me baat karte hain — density se word of mouth chalta hai
- On-site support possible (jo trust banata hai)
- Ek city me case study ban jaye to agli city me sales aasan

**Suggested sequence:** Ambala → Chandigarh/Panchkula → Kurukshetra, Karnal, Yamunanagar
→ Patiala, Ludhiana → Delhi (Mukherjee Nagar, Rajinder Nagar) → Kota, Jaipur, Patna,
Lucknow, Indore

## 3. Field sales process (step by step)

### Step 1: Prospecting
- Google Maps: "study library", "reading room", "library near me" + city
- Instagram: local library pages (bahut active hain)
- Justdial, IndiaMART
- Coaching institutes ke aas-paas physically ghoomo
- Har library ka data sheet me: name, address, owner name, mobile, approx seats, photos

### Step 2: First visit (bina appointment ke jao)
**Timing:** 11 AM - 1 PM ya 4-6 PM (peak nahi, owner free hota hai)

**Opening line (yaad kar lo):**
> "Namaste sir, main [naam]. Main libraries ke liye ek software banata hun.
> Ek minute ka sawaal — abhi aap students ka record kaise rakhte ho?"

Owner register/Excel dikhayega. Ab tum 80% jeet chuke ho — usne apna problem khud
dikha diya.

**Follow-up sawaal:**
- "Pichle mahine kitna due reh gaya tha?"
- "Reminder kaise bhejte ho? Kitna time lagta hai?"
- "Ye seat kis shift me kiski hai, ye kaise pata karte ho?"

### Step 3: Demo (5 minute, phone pe)
**Kabhi feature list mat batao. Uska hi scenario dikhao.**

```
"Sir, aapki library ka naam kya hai? [type karke dikhao]
Kitni seats hain? 90? [90 seats generate karo, seat map dikhao]
Ye dekho — green matlab khaali, red matlab bhari. Morning shift dabao —
sirf morning ki seats dikhengi.

Ab ek student add karte hain. Naam? [unka koi student ka naam lo]
Seat S-24, morning shift, 1 mahina, ₹800. Save.

[Phone nikalo] Dekho, student ke number pe WhatsApp chala gaya —
'welcome, seat S-24, valid till 5 October'.

Aur 5 October se 5 din pehle — automatic reminder chala jayega. Aapko kuch nahi
karna. Aur ye [dashboard] — aaj kitna collect hua, kitna due hai, sab yahan."
```

**Demo ke rules:**
- 5 minute se zyada nahi
- Unka data use karo, dummy data nahi
- Ek WhatsApp message unke apne number pe bhejo — ye sabse strong moment hai
- Feature ki jagah "aapka time bachega" / "paisa nahi doobega" bolo

### Step 4: Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Register se hi kaam chal jaata hai" | "Bilkul sir. Bas ek sawaal — pichle mahine kitne students ki fees miss hui? Agar 3 students ka bhi ₹800 miss hua to ₹2,400 gaya. Ye ₹499 ka hai." |
| "Mehenga hai" | "Ek student ki mahine ki fees se bhi kam hai sir. Aur 14 din free hai — paise tab dena jab fayda dikhe." |
| "Mujhe computer nahi aata" | "Phone chala lete ho? Bas itna hi hai. Aur main khud aakar aapko sikhaunga. Aapka pura purana data bhi main daal dunga." |
| "Data safe hai?" | "Roz automatic backup hota hai. Aur jab chahein pura data Excel me download kar sakte ho — data aapka hai, hamara nahi." |
| "Staff nahi seekh payega" | "Hindi me hai poora. Aur main aapke staff ko 1 ghanta training dunga free." |
| "Sochke batata hun" | "Bilkul sir. Ek kaam karte hain — abhi 5 minute me setup kar deta hun, 14 din free chalao. Pasand na aaye to delete kar dena." (**Yahi close hai** — soch ne mat do, setup kar do) |
| "Pehle bhi software liya tha, chala nahi" | "Kaunsa? Kya problem thi? [suno] Isliye hi main khud aaya hun — main aapke saath setup karunga aur mera number hamesha khula hai." |

### Step 5: Close = setup, not signature
**Sabse important rule:** Wahin baith kar signup karwao aur 5 min me library setup kar do.
"Baad me dekhta hun" = dead lead. Trial start ho gaya to 40% convert honge.

### Step 6: Onboarding (24-48 hours)
- Same day: WhatsApp pe welcome + video link
- Day 1: unka purana Excel/register maango → **tum khud data daalo** (ye sabse bada
  differentiator hai)
- Day 2: 30 min call/visit — staff ko training
- Day 3: check karo ki pehla payment entry hua ya nahi
- Week 1: 2 check-in calls
- Week 2: "kya problem aa rahi hai?" call

**Activation metric:** Owner ne 10 students add kiye + 5 payments entry kiye + 1 reminder
bheja = activated. Activated customers ka conversion 3x hota hai.

aur kabhi churn nahi karega.

## 8. Support = retention

- **WhatsApp support** (email nahi, India me koi email nahi padhta)
- Response time: 2 ghante within business hours
- Har naye customer ko founder ka personal number do (pehle 100 customers tak)
- Har mahine ek "kaise chal raha hai" message
- Bug report aaye to 48 ghante me fix + wapas batao ("aapki problem fix ho gayi")
  — ye customer ko fan bana deta hai

## 9. Churn prevention

**Early warning signals (dashboard pe track karo):**
- 7 din se login nahi kiya
- Students add karna band kar diya
- Payment entry ruk gaya
- Support ticket unresolved

**Action:** turant phone karo. "Sir koi problem hai kya?" 60% cases me chhoti si
confusion hoti hai jo 5 min me solve ho jaati hai.

**Exit interview:** har churn pe poochho "kyun chhoda?" — ye tumhara sabse valuable
product feedback hai.
